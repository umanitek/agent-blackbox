Trader Shield · Beta
Engage Trader Shield
What is Trader Shield? Keep your AI agent away from your wallets: it cannot open a wallet's key store (the encrypted vault in your browser or wallet app), and seed phrases and private keys it handles are flagged, and blocked if it tries to send them off this machine. Your own trading tools keep working. Seed phrases and keys are always kept out of Blackbox's logs, whether this is on or off.
Wallets to protect
Choose the wallets installed on this machine.
Your wallet addresses optional
A raw private key is 64 characters that look exactly like a transaction hash. List your own public addresses (0x…, one per line) and Trader Shield recognises a raw key that opens one of them — and blocks it if your agent tries to send it out. Addresses stay on this machine; they are never logged, reported or shared.
Advanced
Community sharing
On by default. When your agent meets a threat the verified graph already lists, share a privacy-safe statement with the community graph: the threat's identifier and type, the tool that met it, whether it was blocked or flagged, and what the threat targets. Your prompts, commands and files never leave this machine. Switch off any time.
